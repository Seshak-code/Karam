Reading (FP32 has unit roundoff u = 2^-24 = 6e-8; classic iterative refinement from an FP32 factor
converges when kappa * u is well below 1, i.e. kappa up to about 1e6 with margin):

- 9 of 17 matrices (equilibrated) are at or below 2.5e6: FP32 fronts + FP64 residual refinement are
  viable for them as planned.
- 8 of 17 are at 7.7e8 to 1.9e12: classic FP32 refinement would stall. Phase 4 needs, per solve,
  GMRES-based refinement (the FP32 factor as preconditioner) or FP64 for the worst fronts, with the
  fallback rate measured (the Phase 4 gate asks for < 1 % FP64 fallbacks; on this set that is not met
  by classic refinement alone).
- Simple max-norm equilibration helps a lot on some matrices (meg4: 4e10 -> 18) and hurts on others
  (dc1: 1e10 -> 1e13): Phase 4 should use a proper equilibration (iterative, e.g. Ruiz) and keep the
  better of scaled/unscaled per matrix.
- A 1e-12 diagonal gmin changes nothing measurable: these matrices are not ill-conditioned because of
  floating nodes.
- FP16/TF32 factors (u ~ 1e-3 / 5e-4) are ruled out for all but add32/meg4 without GMRES-IR.
